# 📒 CreditBook — Digital Ledger & Credit Tracker

**CreditBook** is a lightweight digital *Khata* (ledger) application built for small shopkeepers, freelancers, and local businesses to replace paper credit notebooks with accurate, real-time balance tracking.

🔗 **Live Demo:** [https://afsalcricket-a11y.github.io/CreditBook/](https://afsalcricket-a11y.github.io/CreditBook/)

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Customer Ledger Accounts:** Create individual accounts for customers or suppliers with contact details and running balances.
- **Credit & Payment Entries:** Log money given (*Credit / Udhar*) and money received (*Payment / Jama*) with timestamps and notes.
- **Automatic Balance Calculation:** Dynamically computes net receivables, net payables, and per-customer outstanding dues.
- **Transaction Audit History:** View a chronological log of all entries for complete transparency and dispute-free settlement.
- **Mobile-First Workflow:** Designed for fast one-handed entry on mobile devices as well as desktop browsers.
